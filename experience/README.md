# Roby's Cinematic Experience

Premium scroll-driven demo page for the existing Roby's Coffee House site.

## Brand fidelity contract

The visual identity is not generated or reinterpreted.

Immutable anchors:

- `../src/brand/robys-header-master-v1.svg` — owner-approved header logo.
- `../src/brand/robys-compact-master-v1.svg` — owner-approved compact logo.
- `../src/brand/robys-mark-master-v1.svg` — owner-approved brand mark.
- `../src/brand/robys-takeaway-cup-v1.webp` — real Roby's takeaway cup used as the persistent hero object.

Do not redraw, regenerate, replace lettering, or synthesize a new Roby's cup/logo for this experience.

AI/video production may create only the world around the immutable product anchor: light, steam, particles, coffee beans, environments, route motion, camera movement and transitions. Generated frames must not bake in Roby's text or a synthetic logo. Brand text remains HTML/SVG so fidelity, accessibility and localization stay under source control.

## Story invariant

The same real Roby's cup remains recognizable from the first scene to the last. The world changes around it.

1. Arrival — product and brand recognition.
2. Origin — beans and material cues.
3. Energy — heat, pressure and transformation.
4. Moment — steam, light and calm focus.
5. Move — Gazipaşa route and takeaway context.
6. Pair — real menu products join the cup.
7. Takeaway — `Yanına al / Take it with you / Возьми с собой` and menu CTA.

## Production path

The current CSS motion layer is a lightweight prototype. A later cinematic pass may replace environmental motion with optimized frame sequences or short local video segments while keeping the HTML copy, approved assets, mobile fallback, CSP and reduced-motion behavior intact.
