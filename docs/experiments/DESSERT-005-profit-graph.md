# DESSERT-005 — Adaptive Profit Graph

Offline only. Run `node experiments/dessert-005/run.mjs`. Baseline DESSERT-004: 4d123b60033f1ab97d5e9551d48d7d5f40c7030d. No runtime import, live traffic, POS or deployment.

Five catalog nodes (190 TRY each chocolate cake, San Sebastian, Lotus Cheesecake; 180 TRY Caffè Latte; 160 TRY filter coffee), plus no dessert state. These prices come from menu-catalog.js at baseline. Directed substitution edges transfer probability mass from no dessert/other desserts to promoted dessert, or reverse under negative effect. Six dessert-coffee joint edges support conditional coffee attachment and assumed shared packaging savings. All monetary values in integer kuruş. Each session can buy at most one dessert and one coffee; refunds independently remove line revenue and retain incurred costs. Costs/fees, demand, effects, substitution, refunds, savings and context mix are synthetic; no learned real-world coefficients. Two observable synthetic browsing contexts, not personal traits. Three treatments (cake story, hypothetical San Sebastian and Lotus promotions), no production content created for those variants. One 40% balanced pilot per policy, then frozen decisions from observed pilot graph node/edge totals, using contribution lower-bound heuristic (2.13 standard errors, minimum 30 observations/arm); fallback control. No post-pilot outcomes used. AB uses same heuristic to compare one treatment; contextual graph has four candidates, separate estimates per context. Fixed activation costs charged once for each tested promotion, including pilot; sunk at decision. Same random basket draws across policies; each scenario fresh unique seed explicitly excludes DESSERT-004's 1000 seeds. Randomized pilot draws are part of seeded simulation; not proof of statistical independence or real causal effect. No capacity dynamics or inventory counts beyond availability flag, no full business profit, no additional drinks or customer lifetime value. Pilot immediate feedback assumes reconciled outcomes known at decision; real refunds arrive later. Threshold not calibrated for real inference. Stress tests: 23, all 1000 scenarios checked for conservation, fees, node/edge bounds, no lookahead, and stockout. Node CI not executed; same functions executed in isolated JS before saving. Evidence excluded from public integrity manifest under qa.

Results: `qa/evidence/dessert-005/results.json`, `scenarios.jsonl`. Metrics are mean scenario-level delta TRY per 1000 eligible sessions, not expected market return. No rollout recommendation absent real evidence.
